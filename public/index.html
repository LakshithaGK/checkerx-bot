🇧🇷 Brazilian Checkers Rules

1. Mandatory Captures: Jumps are forced! If a capture move is available, normal moves are disabled.
2. Backward Captures: Men pieces can capture both forward and backward.
3. King Ability 👑: Kings can fly across open diagonals, but when capturing, they land immediately behind the captured piece.
